Eduvators Privacy and Safety Audit Standard

The Eduvators AI Tool Guide evaluates every listed application against 4 strict institutional benchmarks prior to publication:

  • 1. FERPA and COPPA Data Isolation : Student text, audio submissions, and student identity records must never be utilized to train public foundation models without explicit Data Protection Agreements.
  • 2. Human in the Loop Pedagogy : Educational generative tools must facilitate critical thinking and prompt verification rather than unattended output generation.
  • 3. WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility Standards : Platforms must accommodate screen readers, keyboard navigation, and high contrast visibility for Universal Design for Learning.
  • 4. Classroom Roster Simplicity : Support for teacher managed guest rooms, Google Workspace, or Microsoft single sign on without unsolicited marketing to minors.
